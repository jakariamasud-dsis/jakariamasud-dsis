# 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Full Stack Software Engineer** with over **10+ years of professional experience** designing and developing **Enterprise Software**, **SaaS Platforms**, **Web Applications**, **Desktop Applications**, **Mobile Apps**, and **Custom Business Solutions**. I specialize in building scalable, secure, and high-performance software that helps businesses streamline operations, improve user experiences, and accelerate digital transformation.

My core expertise includes **Laravel**, **WordPress**, **PHP**, **Python**, **Django**, **JavaScript**, **TypeScript**, **React**, **Next.js**, **Vue.js**, **Node.js**, REST APIs, database design, cloud deployment, and modern software architecture. I enjoy working across the entire product lifecycle—from planning and system architecture to development, deployment, and long-term maintenance.

Over the years, I have successfully delivered **200+ commercial projects** for startups, agencies, SMEs, and enterprise clients across a wide range of industries. My portfolio includes **CRM**, **ERP**, **LMS**, **eCommerce**, **Marketplace**, **Booking Systems**, **SaaS Platforms**, **Desktop Applications**, **Mobile Applications**, and other enterprise-grade solutions.

Beyond development, I'm passionate about writing clean, maintainable code, designing intuitive user experiences, optimizing application performance, and solving complex technical challenges. I continuously explore emerging technologies, including **AI-powered applications**, **automation**, and **modern cloud infrastructure**, to build future-ready digital products.

My goal is simple: **to transform innovative ideas into scalable, reliable, and impactful software solutions that drive real business growth.**
